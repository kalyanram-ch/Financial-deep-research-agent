# Research Report
**Query:** Impact of AI on IT services revenue

### 1. Executive Summary
The impact of AI on IT services revenue is significant, with nearly all enterprise software companies expected to embed generative AI into their products by 2024. This is predicted to lead to a revenue uplift of approximately $10 billion by the end of 2024. Revenue teams using AI have reported 29% higher sales growth compared to their peers. The global IT sector is expected to surge, driven by AI, cloud, and cybersecurity, with AI having a projected compound annual growth rate of 29% from 2024 to 2028.

### 2. Market Overview
The IT services market is undergoing a significant transformation with the adoption of AI. According to Deloitte, 2024 is expected to be a transition year for AI-enabled software tools, with adoption and revenues starting to gain traction. The market is driven by the need for cost savings, service delivery, and improved productivity. The emergence of AI agents capable of autonomous planning and execution of complex workflows is set to revolutionize enterprise and consumer technology.

### 3. Key Players & Performance
Key players in the market are investing heavily in AI, with a focus on optimization and training software, MLOps tool platforms, and AI data services. Top performers are fairly engaged with their cloud service providers (CSPs), but there is room for growth in CSP and other IT service provider relationships. Companies such as Gong, TeamDynamix, and Lenovo are already seeing significant benefits from AI adoption, including faster ticket resolution, ticket deflection, and improved customer experience.

### 4. Trend Analysis
The trend is clearly towards increased adoption of AI in IT services, with 87% of participants either having already deployed AI or planning to do so in the next 24 months. The top use cases include knowledge article suggestion and creation, ticket deflection through virtual support agents, and intelligent ticket triage and classification. There is also a growing focus on agentic AI, which is expected to redefine enterprise security and governance.

### 5. Financial Metrics
* The revenue uplift from AI-enabled software tools is expected to approach a $10 billion run rate by the end of 2024.
* Revenue teams using AI have reported 29% higher sales growth compared to their peers, with a 19% growth rate across all organizations surveyed.
* The global IT sector is expected to surge, driven by AI, cloud, and cybersecurity, with AI having a projected compound annual growth rate of 29% from 2024 to 2028.
* Global public cloud spending is expected to double by 2028 from its 2024 figures.

### 6. Regulatory & Risk Factors
There are concerns around AI-related threats, with 61% of IT leaders reporting increased threats and only 31% feeling confident in managing them. There is also a need for better governance and regulation of AI, with a "two-speed workforce" emerging, where some workers use secure AI tools while others do not. The lack of standardization and transparency in AI decision-making processes is also a risk factor.

### 7. Investment Outlook
The investment outlook for AI in IT services is positive, with a strong signal for future growth. The push for AI, particularly agentic AI, is acting as a countercyclical catalyst, with clients prioritizing AI as an avenue for cost-efficiency and productivity gains. However, there are risks associated with AI adoption, and investors should be aware of these risks and ensure that they are investing in companies with strong governance and regulatory compliance.

### 8. Conclusion
In conclusion, the impact of AI on IT services revenue is significant, with a predicted revenue uplift of $10 billion by the end of 2024. The market is driven by the need for cost savings, service delivery, and improved productivity, and key players are investing heavily in AI. However, there are risks associated with AI adoption, and investors should be aware of these risks and ensure that they are investing in companies with strong governance and regulatory compliance.
